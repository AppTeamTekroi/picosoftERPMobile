# Mobile ERP
## Overview
Mobile ERP is a comprehensive application designed to streamline and simplify your business operations. Perfect for managing sales and inventory, this app empowers users to handle orders, payments, invoices, and more—all from their mobile devices. Whether you're a small business owner or managing a large enterprise, Mobile ERP offers a robust solution to keep your business running smoothly and efficiently.

## Features
1. <b>Order Management:</b>
    * Create Orders: Quickly generate new orders with ease.
    * Order Status: Keep track of your orders and stay updated on their current status.
    * Partial Payments: Accept and manage partial payments seamlessly.
2. <b>Payment Processing::</b>
    * Make Payments: Facilitate quick and secure payments directly through the app.
    * Pending Payments: View outstanding payments and accept remaining balances from customers.
    * Download Invoices: Generate and download invoices for completed transactions.
3. <b>Thermal Printing:</b>
    * Generate Thermal Prints: Produce thermal print receipts and invoices on the go, perfect for physical records and customer receipts.
4. <b>Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP):</b>
    * Comprehensive Management: Integrate and streamline all your business processes, from sales to inventory and payment management, in one unified application.
5. <b>User-Friendly Interface:</b>
   * Intuitive Design: Enjoy a clean and user-friendly interface that makes navigation and operation simple and straightforward.

## Technologies Used:
* <b>Flutter:</b> For building a high-performance, cross-platform mobile application with a smooth and responsive user interface.
* <b>GetX (State Management Tool):</b> For efficient and reactive state management, ensuring a seamless user experience.
* <b>Shared Preferences:</b> To store user settings and preferences locally, allowing for quick access and customization.
* <b>flutter_barcode_scanner:</b> For easy and accurate barcode scanning capabilities, simplifying inventory management.
* <b> blue_thermal_printer:</b> For generating thermal prints directly from the app, enabling quick and convenient receipt and invoice printing.
